Project Overview

This project analyzes revenue data from ASOS to identify trends, patterns, and potential business insights. The analysis focuses on understanding revenue changes over time and interpreting key metrics using Python-based data analysis techniques.

Tools & Skills

  • Tools: Python, pandas, Matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook
  • Skills: Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization

Questions Explored

  • How does ASOS revenue change over time?
  • Are there noticeable growth or decline trends?
  • What patterns can be identified from the revenue data?

Key Insights

  • Identified overall revenue trends across the observed period.
  • Highlighted periods of growth and decline.
  • Used visualizations to support data-driven insights.
